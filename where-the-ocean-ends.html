Where the Ocean Ends

A haunting tale of what remains when the tide goes out and the silence takes hold.

Chapter One

The sea has a way of claiming what isn't its to keep. It starts with the edges of a photograph left too long in the sun, then moves to the wooden planks of a pier that once held the weight of a summer's promise. By the time I returned to Blackwood Cove, I realized it had also claimed the memories I thought I’d buried miles away from the salt and the spray.

Julian was standing there, exactly where the maps said the town ended. He looked like a ghost carved from granite, the Pacific wind tearing at his coat. He didn't turn when the gravel crunched under my boots. He didn't have to. The ocean had told him I was coming long before I even crossed the state line.

"You're late, Elena," he said, his voice a low vibration that matched the churning of the water below. "Seven years late."
