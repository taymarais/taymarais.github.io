THE HEART HAS MANY VOICES.

Pinterest Twitter

An observer of human complexity and the intricate nuances of modern relationships. Writing with a deep, compelling, and magnetic voice, Tay explores the bittersweet journey of healing, the boundaries of identity, and the raw search for authenticity in a world of appearances.

Finding inspiration in the contrast between the stillness and the intensity of music, the author believes the most powerful stories are those that force us to face our own vulnerability. Her novels bring to life deeply flawed characters, marked by consuming passions, who find redemption and danger in one another. Fascinated by the vastness and mysteries of the sea, she translates this atmosphere into the perfect psychological backdrop for her gripping plots.
