The heart has many voices.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, I am an observer of human complexity and the intricate nuances of modern relationships. Writing with a voice that seeks the raw and magnetic, I explore the bittersweet journey of healing, the boundaries of identity, and the raw search for authenticity in a world of appearances.

Finding inspiration in the contrast between the stillness of nature and the intensity of music, I believe the most powerful stories are those that force us to face our own vulnerability. My novels bring to life deeply flawed characters, marked by secrets and consuming passions, who find redemption and danger in one another. Fascinated by the vastness and mysteries of the sea, I translate this atmosphere into the perfect psychological backdrop for my plots.
